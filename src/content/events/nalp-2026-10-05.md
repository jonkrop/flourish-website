intro: >-
  You hear it from partners constantly: "Associates just don't want to work
  anymore." That's not quite true - but something has shifted, and today's
  associates need to be engaged differently to bring their best.


  This session gives PD professionals the skills to coach partners on earning enthusiastic collaboration, not grudging compliance. Drawing on self-determination theory and engagement research, we'll cover practical approaches for making tough asks skillfully, recognizing effort in ways that land, and building relationships of mutual respect - and how to introduce these skills to partners without triggering defensiveness. You'll leave with language and frameworks for the coaching conversations that actually shift how partners lead.
