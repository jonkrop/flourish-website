intro: >
  Law firms work hard to train their lawyers for oral argument, depositions,
  client pitches, and other occasions that call for public speaking.
  Unfortunately, this training doesn't prepare lawyers for the intense
  performance anxiety that often arises. This anxiety can derail a lawyer's
  performance and throw all that training out the window. Luckily, this is
  manageable.


  This interactive program explores simple, effective ways lawyers can prepare psychologically for public speaking, stay calm under pressure, and perform at the highest level. The program draws on mindfulness, positive psychology, and third-wave cognitive therapies.
